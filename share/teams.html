FPLN · Microsoft Teams

Put FPLN in front of your team.

Three ways, from quickest to fanciest: a post you paste into any chat or channel, a header picture for a channel Announcement, and an interactive card with buttons.

1. Paste-in post

Click Copy post, then paste (Ctrl+V) into a Teams chat or a new channel post. The picture and links come with it. No setup.

FPLN: Own the day. A free planner built by cadets, for cadets. What's next and when to leave, quick tasks, an alarm that briefs your day, minute-calling heads-ups and the uniform of the day. Your data stays on your device.

2. Announcement header

In a channel, start a post, choose Announcement, then the picture button on the headline and Upload an image. Teams crops headers differently on computers and phones, so there are two shapes: wide (Teams' banner shape) and 2:1 (shows more). The FPLN name sits in the middle so it survives any crop.

3. Interactive card

A Teams card with buttons: Get FPLN, What's inside (opens six features inside the card; each opens FPLN on that feature) and Open in browser. Teams needs a Workflow to post a card; the steps take about two minutes.

Download card.jsonDownload the steps
  1. Teams › Workflows › Create from blank. Trigger: Manually trigger a flow.
  2. Add Microsoft Teams › Post card in a chat or channel. Post as Flow bot, pick the channel, and paste the card code into Adaptive Card.
  3. Save, then Test › Run flow. The card appears in the channel.
